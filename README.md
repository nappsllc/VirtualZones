# VirtualZones

**Turn one big monitor into several.**

Got an ultrawide or a 4K screen? VirtualZones splits it into zones that behave like separate
monitors. Fullscreen apps stay inside their zone instead of taking over the whole screen, and you
can share just one zone in Zoom, Teams or OBS while keeping the rest of your screen private.

### Why people use it

- **Zones that act like real monitors.** Pick a ready-made layout (25/50/25, halves, thirds) or
  draw your own.
- **Fullscreen stays put.** Videos, presentations and maximized apps fill their zone, not your
  whole display.
- **Share one zone, not your whole screen.** Show exactly what you mean in a meeting, nothing more.
- **Snap windows in a second.** Hold Shift while dragging, or use a keyboard shortcut.
- **Tiny and light.** One small app, about 1 MB of memory, nothing extra to install, no admin
  rights needed.
- **Speaks your language.** Available in 26 languages, with light and dark themes.

It replaces PowerToys FancyZones, RegionToShare and virtual-display splitters with one simple app.
